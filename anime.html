What you can find on AnumBrix

AnumBrix provides anime discovery and tracking features designed to help you find new series and keep track of what you want to watch. Individual anime pages can include descriptions, ratings, genres, episode counts, seasons, studios, and available official or licensed streaming links.

To view a specific anime, use the search on the AnumBrix homepage and select an anime card. Each selected title opens its own anime details page.
